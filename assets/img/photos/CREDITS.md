## Rules for use on this site
- These show the **building and grounds only**. No photograph here shows a
  classroom interior, a library, a laboratory, staff or students.
- Never caption one of these as an interior or an activity. Interiors and
  activities use the marked placeholders in `../placeholders/` until the
  school supplies real ones.
- The grounds are still being developed in these shots (bare earth, young
  saplings). Copy should not describe lawns or mature landscaping.
