What the pictures don't show

A classroom in use is worth more than any photograph

You can see the building here. What you cannot see is how a lesson is taught, or how a teacher handles the child who has not understood.

For that, come on a working day while classes are running. Walk the corridors, look into the rooms, and speak to the teacher your child would have.

No appointment is needed — though a phone call beforehand means someone will be free to take you round properly.
