Admissions

A seat for your child, without the runaround

Two visits is usually all it takes. Everything you need is on this page — and anything that is not, you can ask us on the phone.

Start an enquiry Call +91 89520 21550
The process

Four steps, start to finish

STEP 1

Enquire

Call, send a WhatsApp message or fill the form below. We will tell you which classes have seats and what the fees are.

STEP 2

Visit

Come on a working day. See the classrooms in use, meet the teacher your child would have, and ask whatever you like.

STEP 3

Placement

A short, friendly interaction — a little reading and writing, some oral questions — only so that we place your child in the right class.

STEP 4

Confirm

Hand in the documents, pay the admission fee, and collect the booklist, uniform details and the date term begins.

Age & placement

Which class your child belongs in

Age on 31 March is the usual guide. A child who has studied elsewhere is placed on what they can actually do, not on age alone.

Call us with your child's date of birth and last report card, and we will tell you straight away which class to apply for.

Ask about placement
What to bring

Documents for admission

  • Birth certificate of the child
  • Transfer certificate from the previous school, for Class II upwards
  • Last report card, if the child has studied before
  • Aadhaar of the child and of one parent
  • Passport-size photographs of the child

Bring the originals for verification, and one photocopy of each to leave with the office.

Fees

What it costs

The full fee structure is shared on request — admission fee, tuition, and anything payable once a year, set out in one list so there is nothing to discover later.

Call and we will take you through it, or ask for it in writing by WhatsApp.
