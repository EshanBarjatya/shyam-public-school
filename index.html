Why parents choose us

The things that matter on an ordinary school day

Four commitments, written plainly, so that you can hold us to them.

Classes start on time

A period lost each day is a month lost across the year. The timetable is kept, not approximated.

Nothing left half-taught

A topic is not closed on the day it is introduced. The next class opens with questions on it.

Quiet children get asked

The child who never raises a hand is the one most easily lost. They are called on, gently and often.

Rooted in this village

Families know the staff by name. Children walk into a school that belongs to their own community.

Admissions

Four steps, start to finish

Most families complete the whole process in two visits.

STEP 1

Enquire

Call, message on WhatsApp or send the form. We reply with the classes available and the fee structure.

STEP 2

Visit

Walk the campus on a working day, meet the class teacher and ask your questions in person.

STEP 3

Placement

A short, friendly interaction, so that your child starts in the class that actually suits them.

STEP 4

Confirm

Submit the documents, pay the admission fee, and collect the booklist and uniform details.
